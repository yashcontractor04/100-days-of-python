# Day 7: Hangman

The classic word guessing game, played in the terminal. The computer picks a random word and you guess one letter at a time. Every wrong guess costs one of your 6 lives and draws more of the hangman in ASCII art (pictures made out of text).

If you guess a letter you already tried, you don't lose a life.

The word list and the ASCII art are kept in their own files and imported into `main.py`.

To run it: `python main.py`
